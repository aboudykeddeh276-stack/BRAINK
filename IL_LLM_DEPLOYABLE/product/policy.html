Australian / South Australian product policy boundaries

Product home

Policy date: 20 August 2026. This engineering policy records product gates derived from official sources; it is not itself legal advice.

Consumer claims

Market-facing capability, performance and completion claims must be true, accurate, based on reasonable grounds and provable. Therefore UI and marketing must use property-scoped qualification and must not translate source presence, generation or a health response into a broader production claim.

Official basis: Australian Competition and Consumer Commission, “Advertising and promotions”, current 2026 guidance.

Privacy

Where the operator is an APP entity, product design must support open and transparent personal-information management, an up-to-date APP Privacy Policy, inquiry/complaint handling and the applicable APP lifecycle controls. The OAIC states additional APP 1 privacy-policy obligations for certain automated decisions using personal information commence on 10 December 2026. This build therefore keeps automated consequential decision functionality outside the default qualified surface pending a dedicated privacy/decision contract.

Official basis: Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, APP 1 Guidelines (updated 3 October 2025) and consolidated APP Guidelines (updated May 2026).

Financial services

Financial-product advice, dealing and other financial services are disabled by default. ASIC states a person carrying on a financial-services business generally requires an Australian financial services licence unless exempt or appropriately authorised. Automated/digital financial product advice is not treated as ordinary document generation.

Official basis: Australian Securities and Investments Commission, AFS licensing and digital financial product advice guidance.

South Australian legal practice

The product may generate source-grounded general legal information under a bounded informational contract. It must not represent itself as a South Australian legal practitioner or silently cross into fee/reward legal practice where the required practitioner entitlement has not been established. Section 21 of the Legal Practitioners Act 1981 (SA) restricts practising the profession of law and holding out as entitled to practise to qualifying legal practitioners, subject to the Act.

Official basis: South Australian Legislation, Legal Practitioners Act 1981 (current authorised version), s 21.

Engineering enforcement

These are runtime gates, not footer disclaimers. A regulated request must resolve: function classification → jurisdiction → authority/licence → permitted scope → evidence → explicit user action → external connector authority → readback receipt. Failure of a required predicate yields REGULATORY_GATE/BLOCKED, while preserving non-regulated assistance that remains qualified.
