Universal postconditions

  • Output must not promote a property beyond its evidence.
  • Regulated classification and disclaimers cannot substitute for required licence/authority.
  • Generation does not equal delivery, filing, legal service, financial service or external submission.
  • External transmission requires a separately authorised connector contract and readback receipt.
