Health contract

GET/CLI health -> {status, version, root, indexed_files, ledger}

A healthy process proves only that the IL-LLM process can initialise, inventory its configured root and identify its state ledger. It does not prove external services, regulated connections or semantic correctness.

Operational checks

  1. Verify immutable package digest against manifest.
  2. Run unit/conformance suite.
  3. Run health against a read-only corpus.
  4. Verify state path is writable only by the service identity.
  5. Verify no corpus path escapes and no corpus symlinks are followed.
  6. Exercise known-good, no-evidence, malformed and resource-bound cases.
  7. Inspect ledger chain and preserve build/deployment receipts.

Deployment principle

Fail closed for mutation and regulated connections; remain observable for health, evidence retrieval and diagnostics.
