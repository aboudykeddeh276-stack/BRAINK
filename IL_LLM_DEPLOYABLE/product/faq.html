IL-LLM FAQ

Product home

What is this build?

An isolated IL-LLM integration candidate that performs evidence-scoped local retrieval, admission, orchestration and receipt generation. It does not replace an accepted BRAINK runtime merely because it exists in GitHub.

Does “DONE” mean production-ready?

No. Completion is property-scoped. A generated plan, successful file write, passing health check and production qualification are different states.

What can automation do?

It may execute explicitly authorised, qualified functions such as inventory, retrieval, validation, health checks, deterministic packaging and evidence recording. It must not infer authority from tool visibility or silently activate an unqualified runtime.

Does it provide legal or financial advice?

Not by default. Legal-information/document and financial-information functions require separate contracts and regulatory gating. Connections that amount to regulated practice or financial services remain disabled unless appropriate authority is established.

What happens when evidence is missing?

The operation is routed to an explicit unresolved/blocked state. Missing evidence is not converted into a fabricated answer or a claim that the wider capability does not exist.

How is provenance retained?

Evidence items carry source paths and SHA-256 identities; operation receipts are hash-linked. This is integrity evidence, not an external digital signature or trusted timestamp.
