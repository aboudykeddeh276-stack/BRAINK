Principal risks

  1. False-positive completion: plan construction can label status DONE before steps execute.
  2. Compatibility overclaim: file-presence/content heuristics can pass without behavioral interoperability.
  3. Bounded sampling blind spots: compatibility scans at most 10,000 files, samples 300 files and 24 KB per sampled file; important evidence can be outside the sampled region.
  4. Security coverage gap: reviewed baseline does not itself demonstrate fuzzing, hostile corpus handling, SBOM, dependency/image scanning, secrets scanning, privilege tests, or tamper-evident external anchoring.
  5. Regression coverage gap: smoke execution is not a complete contract/regression suite.
  6. Environment boundary: repository review cannot establish the correctness of uninspected local machines, Drive corpus, private unindexed repositories, runtime processes or deployed services.
