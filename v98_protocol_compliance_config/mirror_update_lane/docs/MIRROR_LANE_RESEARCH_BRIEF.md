# Mirror Update Lane Research Brief

## Objective

The mirror update lane is the formal R&D replication and deployment lane for the V98 protocol-compliance package. Its purpose is to mirror every research, development, standards, protocol, compliance, configuration and deployment-control record into an auditable lane that can be checked independently from the main service-spine path.

## Research basis

The lane uses a current reference pack rather than ungrounded certification language:

- ISO 56001:2024 for innovation-management-system structure.
- ISO/IEC/IEEE 12207:2026 for software life-cycle process structure.
- ISO/IEC 42001:2023 for AI-management governance structure.
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and ISO/IEC 27002:2022 for information-security management and controls.
- ISO/IEC 27018:2025 for cloud PII-processing control alignment.
- ISO/IEC 23894:2023 for AI risk-management structure.
- ISO/IEC 25010:2023 for software quality modeling.
- NIST SSDF 1.1 for secure software-development practices.
- NIST CSF 2.0 for cybersecurity risk framing.
- OWASP ASVS and SAMM for application-security and software-assurance checks.
- SLSA and CycloneDX for supply-chain provenance and BOM/SBOM alignment.

## Research conclusion

The correct mirror lane is not a second product and not a duplicated authority. It is a controlled reflection path: it mirrors the same source documents, evaluates the same rules, writes an independent receipt, reads the volume back and emits a separate handoff package.

## Boundary

The mirror lane does not claim external certification, provider acknowledgement, public DNS deployment, physical VFS mount, or target-host runtime proof until corresponding receipts exist.
