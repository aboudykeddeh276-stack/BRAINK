# KEDDEH Systems Full Product-Umbrella Portfolio

## Portfolio authority

```text
portfolio://keddeh/systems
version: 1.2.0
status: AUTHORITATIVE_ADDITIVE_CAPABILITY_MODEL
```

KEDDEH Systems is a governed portfolio of independent, top-level product umbrellas. Naming an umbrella does not narrow or discard capabilities already established elsewhere in the KEDDEH architecture. Each umbrella inherits the common KEDDEH capability substrate, adds its native specialisation, and exposes bilateral interfaces to every adjacent umbrella that can lawfully or technically extend its market function.

## Governing expansion law

```text
full umbrella capability
=
common KEDDEH substrate
+ native market specialisation
+ inherited adjacent capabilities
+ bilateral interfaces
+ product families
+ runtime/deployment projections
+ legal/regulatory obligations
+ assurance/evidence requirements
```

The following reductions are prohibited:

```text
clearer name → fewer capabilities
specialist function → isolated product
new umbrella → deletion of prior functionality
shared capability → hidden ownership
bilateral reuse → hierarchy or merger
```

# Legal-project and insurance-project usage

The existing legal project is a controlled source and case-study environment for LawPath, FormPath, PlayWrite, Coms, Data/Workbook Systems, Legal/Regulatory Conformity, KEO, and Evidence/Assurance.

ClaimPath may reuse compatible evidence, chronology, document, communication, valuation, dispute, and regulatory structures bilaterally, but it remains an independent insurance umbrella. A legal allegation is not automatically an insurance claim; an insurance assessment is not automatically a legal finding.

Matter-specific facts, allegations, claims, authorities, deadlines, policy terms, assessments, damages, and outcomes remain distinct evidence classes. They may not be promoted into universal legal, insurance, regulatory, scientific, or commercial conclusions without the relevant authority-specific evidence.

# Portfolio evolution rule

```text
new finding or product
→ identify all affected umbrellas
→ preserve existing umbrella capabilities
→ add new native and bilateral capabilities
→ update topology and product registries
→ update regulatory and assurance obligations
→ validate no capability was silently removed
→ preserve the portfolio revision and evidence
```

The portfolio is therefore cumulative and extensible: **more precise naming must result in more complete capability definition, never less.**
